Away from home
Sync and read from anywhere, not just on your home Wi-Fi.
  1. Install Tailscale (free) on this PC and on your phone or tablet.
  2. Sign in to both with the same account, such as your Google account.
  3. While you're home, scan this PC's QR code once in Mangarino (PC › Other ways to connect › Scan QR code).
Tailscale links your own devices privately over the internet. Mangarino uses it by itself when you're out.
